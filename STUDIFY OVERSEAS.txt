STUDIFY OVERSEAS — OFFICIAL WEBSITE BRIEF
1. Core objective
The website should communicate within the first 5–10 seconds:
Who are we? STUDIFY Overseas Consultants.
What do we do? Overseas education counselling, university admissions, scholarships, education loans, documentation, visa guidance and pre-departure support.
Why should a student trust us? Founder-led guidance, experience since 2019, transparent counselling and personalized university selection.
What should the visitor do next? WhatsApp / Call / Book Free Profile Evaluation.
The website must be designed primarily for lead conversion, not merely appearance.

2. Brand positioning
Position STUDIFY as:
Personalized. Transparent. Founder-led overseas education guidance.
Avoid presenting it as:
"We send everyone abroad."
The communication should instead feel like:
We help students choose the right country, course and university based on their profile, career goals and financial situation.
That will age much better as your public reputation grows.

25. Things that should NOT appear on the website
This is important.
Do not publish:
	•	❌ Fake student numbers.
	•	❌ "100% visa success."
	•	❌ "Guaranteed visa."
	•	❌ "Guaranteed scholarship."
	•	❌ "Guaranteed PR."
	•	❌ Fake university partnerships.
	•	❌ University logos without appropriate authorization.
	•	❌ Agent commission structures.
	•	❌ B2B commercial contracts.
	•	❌ Student bank statements.
	•	❌ Loan documents.
	•	❌ Passport details.
	•	❌ CAS/I-20 numbers.
	•	❌ Personal student financial information.
	•	❌ Competitor criticism.
	•	❌ "Other consultants cheat students."
	•	❌ Fake Google reviews.
	•	❌ Purchased testimonials.
	•	❌ False "No.1 in Hyderabad" claims.
This is where good PR protects you.

26. Your positioning versus competitors
Never say:
"Other consultancies are commercial."
Instead:
At STUDIFY, we believe every student's profile is different. Our counselling therefore starts by understanding the student's academic background, financial situation and long-term career goal before recommending options.
You're communicating your difference without attacking anybody.
