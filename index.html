Meet Sai Teja Mudrakola

Sai Teja started his journey in overseas education guidance in 2019 after personally experiencing how confusing university selection, admissions and international education planning can be.

That experience became the foundation for STUDIFY Overseas — with a focus on transparent information, personalized counselling and helping students make informed decisions about their future.

Today, he personally leads STUDIFY's counselling approach and works with students planning higher education across major international study destinations.
